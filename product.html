Good to know

Questions, answered.

Still curious? Write to us a human (usually with soil under their nails) replies within a day.

How does my plant survive shipping? +

Each plant is potted, watered and secured in a soil-lock insert the day it ships. Boxes are plastic-free with breathing holes, for 1–3 days in transit.

What is the 7-day root guarantee? +

If your plant arrives damaged or declines within 7 days, send us a photo a replacement ships free, no return required.

Does the decorative pot come potted? +

Plants ship in their nursery pots; decorative pots ship alongside. Pick "ships potted" at checkout and we pot it for you (10% bundle discount applies).

Can I add a gift note? +

Yes free handwritten cards, and we never include prices in the box. Add your note in the cart before checkout.

I'm a serial plant-killer. Where do I start? +

Start with our Easy Care collection, and read the care card in the box each one is written for the exact plant you received, not a generic species page.

Do you ship internationally? +

Currently EU-wide; soil regulations keep us from shipping live plants overseas. Pots & objects travel worldwide.
